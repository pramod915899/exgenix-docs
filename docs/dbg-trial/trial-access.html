Unified QA Governance View — Prototype Edition​

This platform is a prototype environment designed to demonstrate how Exgenix will unify QA governance across every critical subsystem of a stock exchange — from API-level validation to post-trade surveillance.

The goal of this sandbox is to showcase the intended workflow, visibility, and traceability model, not to represent final production behavior, feature completeness, or performance.

Prototype Capabilities — What You Can Explore​

Within this prototype environment, you will be able to:

  • Experience AI-generated test cases directly from Jira stories using stock-exchange-tuned QA models
  • View end-to-end traceability linking requirements → test cases → execution outcomes → defects
  • Understand test asset structure across multi-layer exchange domains (Gateway, Matching Engine, Risk, Clearing & Settlement, Surveillance)
  • Explore conversational, role-based QA insights designed for Business, Product Owners, Scrum Masters, Developers, and QA Engineers through chat-style governance and reporting

Expectation Alignment (What This Is Not Yet)​

  • Not a final production-ready release
  • Does not represent full functional coverage or real-time performance
  • May contain placeholder logic, limited datasets, and evolving UX/UI
  • Intended primarily for concept validation and feedback collection
